Via helper laptop

Your phone and the laptop can't reach each other directly, usually because both are behind a home router or a mobile network. Their traffic passes through another computer on the internet that offered to help. It works, but it is slower, and the helper may limit how long the connection lasts.

Technical details
Connection is a libp2p circuit relay v2 connection (multiaddr contains /p2p-circuit). Relayed connections are usually limited by the relay (default: 2 minutes, 128 KiB). libp2p tries hole punching (DCUtR) to upgrade to a direct connection.
